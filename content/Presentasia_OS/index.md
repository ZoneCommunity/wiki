# Presentasia OS

**Presentasia OS** (formerly styled as (Presentasia) OS) is a family of PowerPoint OSes created by Presentasia Technologies, written in PowerPoint VBA. It began in May 2012 with Presentasia OS 1.0, and its latest stable release is Presentasia OS 8.0 Boundless.

## Overview

Presentasia OS is a family of nine versions, released between 2012 and 2014. The first three versions arrived in quick succession in 2012: 1.0, 2.0 TreeFrog, and 3.0 SnowBall, with 3.0 being the first version in HD. Versions 4.0 Matrix through 7 make up the Classic era, with 6.0 Squared briefly breaking from the Matrix interface before 7 returned to it. Presentasia OS 8.0 Boundless is the most popular version, with over 2,000 downloads. Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion was shown in design concepts in 2014 but was never released.

## History

Presentasia OS 1.0 was released on May 21, 2012, followed by 1.0.1 on August 1 the same year. Presentasia OS 2.0 TreeFrog followed on August 13, 2012, and Presentasia OS 3.0 SnowBall just two weeks later on August 29, 2012, the first version in HD.

Versions 4.0 Matrix, 5.0 Kaleidoscope, 6.0 Squared, and 7 continued the Classic line. 6.0 Squared introduced the angular-style logo still used today, and briefly used a different desktop icon layout before 7 went back to the Matrix interface.

Presentasia OS 8.0 Boundless became the most popular version, with over 2,000 downloads. An update to 8.0 was planned but canceled early in development.

On June 24, 2014, Presentasia Technologies posted design concepts for Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion, with a new flat design and features from the canceled [Eminent](/Presentasia_OS/Eminent/) project. Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion was never released. On October 1, 2014, Presentasia canceled all of their projects, including OS 9, resigned from the PowerPoint OS Network, and passed it to Midtown Technologies.
