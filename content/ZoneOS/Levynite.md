# Levynite

**Levynite** is a version of ZoneOS, running the ZoneCore core.
It branched off [Mesolite](/ZoneOS/Mesolite/) as a simplified
release version.

## History

Levynite was meant to be a simpler version of ZoneOS Mesolite,
and was originally called ZoneOS Lite. Instead, it faced many
problems throughout development, forcing a new direction to
be taken.

Levynite was picked back up in July 2025, after almost two
months of inactivity.

Eventually, in November 2025, Mesolite and Levynite merged into
[ZoneOS+](/ZoneOS/ZoneOS-Plus/), soon becoming Mesolite again.
