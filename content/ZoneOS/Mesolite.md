# Mesolite

**Mesolite** is the latest version of ZoneOS, running the ZoneCore core.
It is the longest developed ZoneOS version, with the most
features.

## Overview

Mesolite features a comprehensive list of application features
that exist

Underneath it is the Surface System, which is ZoneOS's class-based
VBA UI toolkit for creating ZoneOS applications. It also comes with
ZoneFS, its filesystem, and other features.

## History

In August of 2024, ZoneOS Mesolite originally began development as
an experimental PPTOS

In late 2025, ZoneCore ran in both Mesolite and
[Levynite](/ZoneOS/Levynite/). Levynite branched off Mesolite as
a simplified release version, but it gained complex experimental
features and got buggy, while Mesolite lagged behind in features.
So, they merged into [ZoneOS+](/ZoneOS/ZoneOS-Plus/), which then
was then stripped of some Levynite UI elements and branded back
into Mesolite again.

In Novemeber of 2026, ZoneOS Mesolite's development was picked
back up again after months of inactivity, entering a codename "R2"
phase of development.

## Apps and features

- App Launcher, Edit, Code, and Settings
- Theme Creator, Setup, Login, and Welcome
- Countdown and Timer, MsgBox Demo, and Surface Demo
- Language Switcher, with support for more and more languages over time
