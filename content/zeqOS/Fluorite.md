# zeqOS Fluorite

**zeqOS Fluorite** was the first known version of zeqOS to create applications fully in VBA. It also had some form of theme engine.

Fluorite was never released, and was likely replaced by [zeqOS Natrolite](/zeqOS/Natrolite/). Screenshots of Fluorite show Bubbly in the taskbar, so Fluorite might have included a working Bubbly.
