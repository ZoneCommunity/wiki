# Eminent

**Eminent** was a project by Presentasia Technologies. It was announced in October 2013 as the successor to Presentasia OS 8, and was set to release in Spring 2014. It was described as a very simple OS with only six applications. It was canceled before release, and its features were moved to Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion.

## Development

Eminent's development was supposed to come before Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion. Screenshots of Eminent were shared on January 6, 2014. On April 16, 2014, Presentasia estimated that Eminent would release in mid-May. On April 30, 2014, Presentasia was still building the Eminent page on presentasia.net. After Eminent was canceled, OS 9's development started right away instead of waiting for Eminent's release.

## Features

Eminent was to include offline integration and a Music app, both of which were moved to Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion.

## Cancellation

On June 24, 2014, Presentasia announced that Eminent was officially canceled, and that Presentasia OS 9 had started development. Its features were moved to Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion.
