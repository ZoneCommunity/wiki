# Mesolite apps

[Mesolite](/ZoneOS/Mesolite/) ships with a plethora of
testing apps. They include:

- App Launcher
- Edit
- Settings
- Theme Creator
- Setup
- Login
- Welcome
- Countdown / Timer
- MsgBox Demo
- Surface Demo
- Language Switcher

The Language Switcher covers en-US, es-ES, pt-BR, and ja-JP.
