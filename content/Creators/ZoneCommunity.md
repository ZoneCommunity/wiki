# ZoneCommunity

ZoneCommunity is the organization that develops ZoneOS, a PPTOS written in PowerPoint VBA.

## Overview

ZoneCommunity is the copyright holder of projects such as ZoneOS and parallel.
It maintains and develops ZoneOS and ZoneCore, along with its website. It also runs the
PowerPoint OS Network.

## History

ZoneCommunity originally started in 2021, under the name GlassOS Community.
However, with the cancellation of GlassOS and the start of ZoneOS, it rebranded into
ZoneCommunity in March of 2022.
