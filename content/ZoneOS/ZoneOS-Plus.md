# ZoneOS+

**ZoneOS+** is the merger of [Mesolite](/ZoneOS/Mesolite/) and
[Levynite](/ZoneOS/Levynite/), running the ZoneCore core.

## History

In late 2025, ZoneCore ran in both Mesolite and Levynite.
Levynite branched off Mesolite as a simplified release version,
but it gained complex experimental features and got buggy, while
Mesolite lagged in features. So they merged into ZoneOS+.

After some Levynite UI elements were stripped out, ZoneOS+ became
Mesolite again.
