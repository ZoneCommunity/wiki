# Presentasia OS 1.0

Presentasia OS 1.0 (also known as (Presentasia) OS 1.0, or simply Presentasia) was the first OS by Presentasia Technologies. A preview was shown on May 11, 2012, in the Presentasia Trailer video (which has since been made private by the owner). It was released on May 21, 2012, followed by 1.0.1, released on August 1 the same year.

## Applications and features

Presentasia OS 1.0 contained basic applications like Internet Atlas, Writasia, Store, Office, Files, Disc, Apps, Games, and more.

## 1.0.1

The 1.0.1 update was released to address errors with hyperlinks and to rename Internet Escort to Internet Atlas.
