# Presentasia Technologies

Presentasia Technologies is the organization that developed Presentasia OS, an iconic PowerPoint OS of the 2010s.

## Overview

Presentasia Technologies is the organization that developed Presentasia OS, an iconic PowerPoint OS of the 2010s. It also worked on Eminent, a project whose features were moved to Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion after Eminent was canceled.

## History

Presentasia Technologies was founded on May 11, 2012. It developed Presentasia OS from 2012 to 2014, releasing eight versions, with Presentasia OS 8.0 Boundless as the latest.

On October 1, 2014, Presentasia canceled all of their projects, including Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion, resigned from the PowerPoint OS Network, and passed it to Midtown Technologies.
