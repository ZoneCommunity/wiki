## Notes

- [Presentasia OS 8.0 Boundless](/Presentasia_OS/8.0/) is the latest stable release, and the most popular version, with over 2,000 downloads.
- [Presentasia OS 9 Pavilion](/Presentasia_OS/9/) was shown in design concepts in 2014 but was never released.
- [Presentasia OS 3.0 SnowBall](/Presentasia_OS/3.0/) was the first version in HD.
- [Presentasia OS 6.0 Squared](/Presentasia_OS/6.0/) introduced the angular-style logo.
